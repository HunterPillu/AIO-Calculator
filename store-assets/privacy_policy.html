Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All-in-One Calculator handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

All-in-One Calculator is designed primarily to perform calculations on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the core calculator features.

Depending on the version and features you use, the app may process information that you enter into calculators, such as loan amounts, investment amounts, interest rates, dates, and other calculation inputs. This information is used to provide the requested calculation.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into the app is used to:

  • Perform calculations and display results.
  • Provide calculation history, favorites, or other features when enabled.
  • Improve app reliability and functionality where applicable.

3. Data Storage

Where the app provides local history or saved calculations, that information may be stored locally on your device. The app does not need to upload calculator inputs to a server for ordinary calculations.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for purposes such as app distribution, crash reporting, analytics, advertising, or other optional functionality if those services are enabled in a particular release.

If third-party services are added, this Privacy Policy and the app's applicable disclosures should be updated to accurately describe the information shared with those services.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using the app or remove locally stored app data through your device settings. If the app provides a history or saved-data deletion feature, you can also use that feature to remove applicable local data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be made available with a revised “Last updated” date.
